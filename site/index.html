What is Direct Primary Care?

A membership model built around your relationship with your doctor

You pay one transparent monthly fee for expanded access to your physician — no per-visit co-pays, no insurance middleman standing between you and the care you need.

  • Office visits & virtual visits
  • Direct email & texting access to your doctor
  • Same-day / next-day sick appointments
  • Basic in-office testing (urinalysis, strep, flu, COVID)
  • Women's health exams & EKGs
  • Transparent pricing, no surprise bills
