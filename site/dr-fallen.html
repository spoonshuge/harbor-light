Amy Fallen

“Patients deserve better” than a rushed 15-minute appointment.

Dr. Fallen transitioned to direct primary care after seeing firsthand how traditional hospital settings left no room for the kind of time patients actually need. She built her practice around personalized, preventative care and a true partnership with every patient — with particular expertise in chronic disease management, midlife transitions, women's health, and menopause management.
