Everything you're wondering
about Direct Primary Care

DPC gives you real time with your physician — 30 to 60 minute visits instead of a rushed 15 minutes — plus direct texting and email access, same/next-day sick visits, and a doctor who actually knows your history.

No. DPC operates independently from insurance. Your monthly membership covers direct access to your physician for office and virtual visits; you'll still want insurance to cover labs, imaging, hospitalizations, and specialist care.

Yes. Medicare patients are welcome to join Harbor Light. Tests and services that Medicare normally covers remain covered by Medicare — your membership simply adds direct access to your physician on top of that.

Membership is billed monthly by age:

  • Ages 18–24: $100/month
  • Ages 25–44: $135/month
  • Ages 45–64: $155/month
  • Ages 65+: $175/month

Office visits, virtual visits, direct email/texting access, point-of-care testing (urinalysis, strep, flu, COVID), women's health exams, EKGs, and form completion. Labs, imaging, and specialist referrals are billed separately, and insurance benefits still apply to those.

They're similar in spirit — both prioritize time and access — but DPC keeps membership costs low by not billing insurance at all for office visits, which is why our pricing stays well below typical concierge retainers.
